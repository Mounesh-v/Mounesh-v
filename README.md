## About me

I'm a final-stage **B.E. student in Computer Science and Engineering at BMS Institute of Technology and Management (BMSIT), Bangalore**, and a full-stack developer who likes taking a project all the way from idea to a live URL.

Most of my work is on the **MERN stack and Next.js**: REST APIs, JWT auth with role-based access, database design, and production deployment. Lately I've been building **AI-powered features**, including vector search, embeddings and image-based search, and I enjoy making them fast and usable rather than just demo-ready.
